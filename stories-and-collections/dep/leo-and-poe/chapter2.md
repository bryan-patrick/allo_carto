Leo and Poe

Chapter 2: I Am a Raven.

Good evening. I am a raven. My name is Poe. It is a good name for a raven.

The name was given to me by a woman who lives in Limoilou. In the evenings, I sit with her on her balcony while she read books to me. I like books.

Does she know I understand her? Yes, she knew. I understand every word.

I bring her trinkets and she hangs them on her door for decoration. Colorful leaves, coins, lost keys, all kinds of things. It was the price of a good story.

She thought is was especially fun when I would bring her money.

It is rare to find the beautiful paper on the ground, but I am smart enough to pick it up when I see it.

I have some hidden around the city even now. It is for special trades.

How do I know what money is? Ravens are cunning birds. We like to trade. We understand money very well.

I am 27 years old. They say I am old for a raven. I don't listen to such nonsense.

She used to sit outside her store while reading a book. She would read in the summer, she would read in the winter.

I like to watch people for some time before I make myself known. I keep my distance. Not everyone is nice. I am a raven. Grand corbeau. Sharp and cunning.

Everyday this man did the same thing. He would enter the bookstore at 7:30am, exactly. Sometimes mornings are beautiful at this time. The clouds obscure the sun like a lampshade as it rises over the plateua. Us birds enjoy the morning.

I'm not sure why I was interested in this man. There was something so calm about him. His movements. The way he walked around.
